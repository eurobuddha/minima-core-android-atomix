Ethereum submission recovery parity: standalone AtomiX0.1.78/code178

Problem and behavior
The old process-local nonce counter could forget a signed send after restart or a
lost RPC response, and its two-minute timeout could reuse that nonce for another
operation. Reused the exact EthereumTransactions.java from Pandamonium9eec40a,
integrated AtomiX EthRpc/EthHtlc, EthTx with only its standalone error string, and
adapt-pandamonium-ethereum-submissions.py's saved-counter-leg timelock rule.
The journal saves signed public transactions before broadcasting and retains nonce
ownership across restart, endpoint changes and uncertain outcomes. A stable lock,
withdraw or refund operation retries its original intent; fee replacement retains
nonce/payment/calldata and stays inside the caller's reviewed fee bounds. Missing
or malformed RPC envelopes cannot supply a nonce or acknowledge a send. Responses
are bounded at4MiB. Both Activity and background Service initialize the same private
SQLite journal before constructing their clients. The existing no-backup manifest
and IPC key derivation remain. No private keys are stored in this journal.
Unknown manual sends copy their full actual transaction hash and state that the
outcome is unknown. They are not presented as rejected sends. An automated responder
retry retains the persisted timelock; an expired saved lock cannot create a new lock.
