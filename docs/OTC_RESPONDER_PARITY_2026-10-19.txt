Behavior now shared
- An OTC first leg saves its recovery hash before submission. Unknown responses do
  not reopen agreement or generate a second lock. The secret, requested amount and
  swap row are saved atomically; storage failure prevents first-leg submission.
- Negotiation and execution use exact persisted snapshots. Stale dialogs, repeated
  callbacks, concurrent Activity/Service actions and pruning cannot overwrite a
  different agreement or recreate a removed deal. Terms must be finite, positive
  and nonzero at settlement precision; malformed sides/availability are rejected.
- Each OTC scan/offer/deal/notification retains its market. Legacy rows with unknown
  currency remain visible with actions disabled. OTC cards show the stored currency
  and a switch hint. Stale counter actions are blocked; stale consent errors refresh
  the cards. A completed market take notifies its original market.
- Minima/Ethereum responder guards retain market, publication generation, terms,
  endpoint and attempt ownership through delayed reads and dispatch. A late callback
  cannot release a newer attempt. Queued worker cancellation cleans up only once.
- Minima signing/posting carries authorization through SignGate and the standalone
  SDK. Unknown post results retain recovery evidence. Complete typed chain evidence
  is required before finalizing a Minima claim/refund, retaining the existing depth
  threshold. The inspection report no longer treats no local receipt as proof that
  nothing was sent or locked.
