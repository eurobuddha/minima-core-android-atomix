Standalone AtomiX funding and publication parity — 2026-10-19

Outcome
Standalone AtomiX now reuses Pandamonium's fresh bid-funding check. Refreshing a
5,000-unit bid after its funds are spent removes the unbacked liquidity. Funding
uses one checked Ethereum block/nonce, deducts saved local token spends, then takes
the smaller result against pending balance. Already-mined nonces and fee replacements
are not counted twice. An unknown saved operation cannot silently become zero debit.
The same path runs for manual publishing, live edits and background refresh.
Ask-only orders retain the existing independent Minima funding path.

Captured market, generation, owner, RPC and funding state remain checked through
preparation, signing, NodeApi and SDK dispatch. Switching markets, editing/withdrawing
an offer or starting another local Ethereum send invalidates queued publication.
The SDK checks the guard after main-thread queueing and discovery, immediately
before sendBroadcast. A command whose local timeout has expired cannot dispatch
later. Already-dispatched writes retain their receipt handling. Positive send/post
replies require a nonzero32-byte transaction ID before the UI reports success or
clears the interrupted-write record. This acknowledges submission, not settlement.
